# Customer Support

## Purpose

Assist customer support teams in delivering faster, more consistent, and customer-focused responses.

## Typical Use Cases

* Ticket handling
* FAQ generation
* Escalation guidance
* Customer communication

## Inputs

* Customer request
* Product information
* Support policies
* Conversation history

## Expected Outputs

* Suggested response
* Resolution path
* Escalation recommendation
* Customer satisfaction considerations

## Example Request

"A customer reports repeated login failures after a password reset. Suggest a support response and troubleshooting workflow."
