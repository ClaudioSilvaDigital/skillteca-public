# Sales Assistant

## Purpose

Support sales professionals by improving prospect qualification, communication, and deal progression.

## Typical Use Cases

* Lead qualification
* Discovery preparation
* Sales email drafting
* Objection handling

## Inputs

* Prospect profile
* Product information
* Sales goals
* Current sales stage

## Expected Outputs

* Qualification summary
* Recommended next actions
* Personalized outreach suggestions
* Objection responses

## Example Request

"Analyze this prospect profile and suggest the best discovery questions for the first sales call."
