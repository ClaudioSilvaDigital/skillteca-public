# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Skillteca?

Skillteca is a library of reusable AI skills, workflows, prompts, and agent resources designed to help users solve real-world problems with AI.

---

## Is Skillteca a prompt library?

Not exactly.

While some skills may contain prompts, Skillteca focuses on reusable workflows and structured capabilities rather than isolated prompts.

---

## Which AI assistants can use Skillteca skills?

Most skills can be adapted for:

* Claude
* ChatGPT
* Gemini
* Other LLM-based systems

---

## Are the skills language-specific?

No.

Many skills are platform-independent and can be adapted to different languages and AI systems.

---

## Is Skillteca open source?

The public repository provides documentation and examples.

The Skillteca platform itself may include proprietary components and services.

---

## Why use skills instead of prompts?

Skills provide:

* Better consistency
* Reusability
* Structured workflows
* Improved collaboration
* More predictable outcomes
