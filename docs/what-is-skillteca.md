# What is Skillteca?

## Overview

Skillteca is a multilingual library of reusable AI skills, workflows, prompts, and agent resources.

Its purpose is to help users discover, share, and reuse practical AI capabilities across different AI platforms, including Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other large language models.

## Why Skillteca Exists

Most AI prompt collections focus on isolated prompts designed for a single interaction.

Skillteca takes a different approach.

Instead of organizing content as standalone prompts, Skillteca focuses on reusable skills that represent repeatable workflows, business processes, and problem-solving frameworks.

These skills can be adapted to different AI assistants and real-world scenarios.

## Core Principles

### Reusability

Skills are designed to be reused across multiple projects, teams, and AI platforms.

### Practicality

Every skill should help solve a real-world problem or support a specific business objective.

### Human Curation

Skills are organized and reviewed to improve discoverability and usefulness.

### Platform Independence

Skills can be adapted for Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI systems.
